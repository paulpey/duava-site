Duava Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Duava is a personal screen-time budgeting app for iPhone. This policy is short because Duava collects nothing.

What Duava stores, and where

Everything Duava knows lives on your device, in the app's own private storage:

  • the apps you chose to budget, as opaque Screen Time tokens, and the labels you typed for them;
  • your daily minute budgets and pacing settings;
  • your three written goals;
  • your check-in answers and a per-day history of which budget thresholds were reached;
  • a technical log of the app's own background events, used only for troubleshooting inside the app.

None of this leaves your device. Duava has no account system, no server, no analytics, no advertising, and makes no network connections.

Screen Time (Family Controls)

Duava uses Apple's Screen Time framework, with your explicit authorization, to count usage of the apps you selected and to shield them once a budget is spent. Apple's design means Duava never receives your actual usage data or the real names of the apps; it only receives notifications when a usage threshold you configured is crossed. This information is used solely to provide the budgeting features and is never shared with anyone.

Notifications

With your permission, Duava sends local notifications on your device when a budget is nearly spent, when you tap "Check in" on a shield, and for reminders you set yourself. These are generated on the device; no push notification service is involved.

Data deletion

Use "Reset all data" in Duava's Settings, or delete the app. Either removes everything Duava stored.

Children

Duava is a tool for managing your own device. It is not a parental-control product and does not manage other people's devices.

Changes

If a future version of Duava introduces optional online features, this policy will be updated before those features ship, and the app will say clearly what is sent and why.
